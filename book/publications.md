## Monaural Multi-Speaker Speech Separation Using Efficient Transformer Model

**S. Rijal**, R. Neupane, S. P. Mainali, S. K. Regmi, and S. Maharjan

_ICTCEEL 2023_ (pp. 41–45)

Built a speech separation model with an efficient Transformer-based architecture inspired by the Perceiver model. Designed a novel architecture for monaural multi-speaker speech separation, achieving an SI-SNRi score of 10.5 dB on the WSJ0-2mix benchmark.
