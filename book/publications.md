## Mendel292 at SemEval-2026 Task 4: Disentangled Narrative Embeddings for Story Similarity

Mauricio Gruppi, **Sankalpa Rijal**, and Justin Debenedetto

_Proceedings of the 20th International Workshop on Semantic Evaluation (SemEval 2026)_ (pp. 2575–2582)

Developed a narrative encoder that separates story representations into subspaces for abstract theme, course of action, and outcome. The system combines pretrained sentence embeddings, Bi-LSTM projection layers, triplet-margin learning, backtranslation, and weak supervision from narrative clustering for few-shot story-similarity modeling.

📄 [Read in the ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/2026.semeval-1.324/)

## Monaural Multi-Speaker Speech Separation Using Efficient Transformer Model

**S. Rijal**, R. Neupane, S. P. Mainali, S. K. Regmi, and S. Maharjan

_ICTCEEL 2023_ (pp. 41–45)

Built a speech separation model with an efficient Transformer-based architecture inspired by the Perceiver model. Designed a novel architecture for monaural multi-speaker speech separation, achieving an SI-SNRi score of 10.5 dB on the WSJ0-2mix benchmark.

📄 [View PDF](https://ictceel.khwopaconference.com/uploads/conferencepaper/fullpaper/1044.pdf)
