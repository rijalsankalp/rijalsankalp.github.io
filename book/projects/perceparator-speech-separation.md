## Overview

Built a speech separation model with an efficient Transformer-based architecture inspired by the Perceiver model. Designed a novel architecture for monaural multi-speaker speech separation, evaluated on standard benchmarks.

## Technical Details

1. **Novel Architecture:** Designed and implemented a novel Transformer architecture based on the Perceiver model, optimized for computational efficiency while maintaining separation quality.

2. **Benchmark Performance:** Achieved an SI-SNRi score of 10.5 dB on the WSJ0-2mix benchmark dataset, a standard evaluation set for speech separation research.

3. **Literature Review:** Conducted extensive literature review of speech separation methods, from mask-based to end-to-end approaches, to inform architectural decisions.

4. **Publication:** This work resulted in a published paper at ICTCEEL 2023: _"Monaural Multi-Speaker Speech Separation Using Efficient Transformer Model"_.
